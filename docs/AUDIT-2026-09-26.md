## 🟠 O'RTA (mantiqiy buglar / bog'liqliklar)

### 🔵 S1 (YANGI, ENG MUHIM DIZAYN). `isUnrestricted` — **fail-open** scope
`auth/scope.ts` — eski mantiq: "access yozuvlari BO'LMASA → cheksiz (unrestricted)".
Ya'ni har bir scoped rol (SALES_MANAGER, SERVICE_STAFF...) uchun access
biriktirish **esdan chiqsa**, u avtomatik **butun tarmoqni** ko'rardi.
**Jonli tasdiq:** bazadagi `manager@crm.local` 0 access bilan **hamma** filial,
lead, inventory'ni ko'rdi.

Endi **fail-closed**: faqat SUPER_ADMIN unrestricted; boshqa rol uchun bo'sh grant
→ **hech narsa** ko'rinmaydi. `canAccessScope` ham fail-closed (har bir grant
dimensioni bo'yicha ijobiy moslik talab qiladi).
**Jonli tasdiq:** 0-access manager endi leads=0, vehicles=0, dashboard=0.

> Bu spec §5/§73 tamoyiliga muvofiq ("scoped rol faqat o'z ruxsatiga ega narsani
> ko'radi"). Seed ham endi re-seed'da access'ni **tiklaydi** (self-healing).

### ✅ M5. `findOrCreateCustomer` — race condition
`LeadPhone.phone` unique qilindi (schema). Lekin `findFirst` + `create` orasidagi
race'da loser `P2002` olib **butun transaction'ni yiqitardi** (webhook'da 500).
Endi `P2002` ushlanadi va qayta o'qiladi (idempotent, race-safe).

### ✅ M1. `POST /api/leads/:id/result` — "No Answer → follow-up majburiy"
Backend endi `NO_ANSWER` / `CLIENT_BUSY` uchun `nextFollowUpAt` bo'lmasa
**400 `FOLLOW_UP_REQUIRED`** qaytaradi (frontend bilan izchil, §14).
**Jonli tasdiq:** `NO_ANSWER` follow-up'siz → 400; follow-up bilan → 200;
`INTERESTED` (majburiy emas) → 200.

### 🔵 M2. `GET /api/sales` — SALES_MANAGER `salesManagerId` bo'yicha
To'g'ri. HEAD_OF_SALES o'z scope'ida hammasini ko'radi — spec'ga mos.

### 🔵 M3. `GET /api/leads` — noto'g'ri enum indamay e'tiborsiz
Debug'ni qiyinlashtiradi, xavfsizlik muammosi emas.

### ✅ M4. Login throttle — per-IP limit qo'shildi
Ikki mustaqil limit: mavjud `ip|email` + yangi **per-IP** (LDAP
`LOGIN_MAX_ATTEMPTS_PER_IP`, default 50). Credential-stuffing endi bloklanadi.
**Jonli tasdiq:** bir IP'dan turli emaillarga 50-urinishda 429.
